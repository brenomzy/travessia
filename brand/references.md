## Where Travessia overlaps

Travessia is fictional, so none of this is a legal or market risk. Treat these as notes for later steps, acted on when it's cheap or makes the work better; the outputs and the system behind them matter more than distance from these brands.

Grouped by the playbook step that has to deal with it. **High** means a reader who knows the other brand would likely make the connection; **medium** means it would add up with other similarities; **low** means shared practice.

### The ones that change current plans

These touch things the playbook or the site already treats as decided or planned.

1. **Wise already sells Travessia's product to Brazilians** (high). It holds BRL, pays out by Pix, is authorised by the Banco Central, markets "A conta global" in Brazil, and Wise Business has a freelancer segment with invoices and payment links. Nubank's Nu Global and Ultravioleta Conta Global hold USD and EUR. The fee and the speed can't carry the strategy (step 2). What none of the five does: the export tax invoice (*nota fiscal de exportação*) and the monthly tax report for the accountant, and none speaks from the receiving freelancer's side.
2. **The bank-versus-Travessia bars on Home** (high). The playbook (step 8) calls them "already a signature". Wise's comparison block, a long green Wise pill against grey bank pills with fees in red, is one of its best-known images, and the Brazil site already compares Wise with card IOF. Keep the idea, change the form.
3. **"Açaí" as a held campaign colour** (high). A deep purple reads as Nubank ("roxinho") to most Brazilians, and Nubank Ultravioleta's 2026 palette is deep purple with brown, sand and tan, which is açaí, castanha and cupuaçu together. Drop it or move it towards wine or near-black, and never pair it with sand or brown.
4. **River landscapes in the photography plan** (high). Mercury's home hero is a misty river valley at dawn, and its March 2026 Mercury Movement art is a canoe of people pulled ashore by a rope. Travessia's grey is even called neblina (mist). Keep river imagery to close, water-level objects, not vistas, fog or people landing in a boat.
5. **A sandy "solimões" for the seam** (medium). Sand #F3EFE6 is a major Nubank surface. A sandy colour inside the seam is fine; as a page background, it moves Travessia towards Nubank.

### Brand by brand

- **Ramp:** structure only. Low: urucum's "primary buttons, one per view" is the same rule as solar's; Ramp's warm off-white surfaces, which Travessia doesn't use.
- **Wise:** the closest on product and on fee-up-front language; high on the calculator, the comparison bars and UI cards over desk still lifes; medium on pale teal next to tapajós and on "borders" in copy.
- **Mercury:** the closest on imagery and tone; high on misty river landscapes, the canoe, and single-component UI vignettes; medium on the cool dark base and calm, craft-first tone.
- **Nubank:** the closest in Brazilian readers' heads; high on any deep purple, on cross-border copy and on anti-bank lines; medium on cards or phones in hand, BRL and Pix in phone UI, and sand surfaces.
- **Stripe:** the closest on the invoice flow and on flowing texture; high on the invoice-to-paid vignette, the wave and payment links; medium on blue-black neutrals, an annual data piece and a hero live counter.

## Wise

### Strategy and messaging

| Layer | What Wise has |
|---|---|
| Founding tension | Taavet Hinrikus (paid in euros in London) and Kristo Käärmann (paid in pounds, with a euro mortgage in Estonia) lost money to bank fees and poor rates, so each month they checked the mid-market rate and swapped money between their accounts. The enemy since 2011 is the FX markup hidden in the exchange rate. |

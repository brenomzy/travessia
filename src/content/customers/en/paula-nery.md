## Before: twelve clients, twelve rates

As an individual, Paula had to log every payment in the carnê-leão at that day's rate. With twelve clients, that took a whole afternoon.

> I import the file, check it and pay. No more lost afternoons.
> — Paula Nery

## After: one file a month

The monthly report comes with date, amount, rate and value in reais in separate columns, in the format the carnê-leão app accepts.

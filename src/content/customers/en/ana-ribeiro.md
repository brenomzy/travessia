## Before: the rate her bank chose

Payments came in as international wires, and the bank converted them the same day, with spread and fees. Ana only found out what she'd received from her statement.

> Now I see the number in reais before I press the button. It sounds small, but it changes the month.
> — Ana Ribeiro

## After: a dollar balance and an alert

The studio pays her invoice by local transfer in the UK, the money lands in her dollar balance, and Ana converts when the rate alert tells her to.

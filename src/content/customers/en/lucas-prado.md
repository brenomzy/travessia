## Before: bank details nobody understood

Agencies asked for an IBAN, then a SWIFT code, then the bank's address. Every new client meant a week of emails before the first payment.

> Now I send a link and that's it. For them it's like paying any other supplier.
> — Lucas Prado

## After: a link and a local account

Every invoice comes with a local eurozone account. The money lands in his euro balance and the export nota fiscal is issued in under two minutes.

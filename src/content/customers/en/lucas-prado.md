## Challenge

Every payment from abroad arrived at a different rate, and the tax invoice was left for later.

## Solution

Invoices through Travessia, with the balance kept in the client's currency and converted when it makes sense.

## Results

The amount in reais shows before converting, and closing the month no longer depends on a spreadsheet.

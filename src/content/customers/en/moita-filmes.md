## Before: a spreadsheet and a lot of email

Júlia Moita started the studio in 2019 with one client in New York. By 2025 they had twelve projects a year outside Brazil, each paid a different way: international wire, a payment platform or card.

At the end of every month she gathered receipts, worked out the rate for each payment and sent it all to the accountant to issue the export tax invoices.

> I spent a whole day every month just collecting paperwork. Now the nota fiscal comes out with the payment.
> — Júlia Moita, partner

## After: every invoice closes its own loop

The studio now sends its invoices in English through Travessia. When a client pays, the conversion to reais shows with the 0.6% fee already taken, and the nota fiscal is issued the same day.

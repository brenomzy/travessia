## Before: four accounts for one studio

Each partner had their own way of getting paid, and at the end of the month someone had to pull it all together to split costs and close the tax invoices.

> Now we all look at the same balance and nobody has to chase anybody.
> — Estúdio Caju

## After: one account, four logins

On the Studio plan, all four issue invoices from the same account and the accountant follows along with read-only access.

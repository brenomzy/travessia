## 1. What this site is

A demo site for a fictional company, built to test Source by Webflow. It isn't a bank, moves no money and stores no data.

## 2. Nothing here is an offer

Plans, fees and timings describe a made-up product. No account can be opened and no payment can be made.

## 3. Examples and numbers

Rates, amounts and customer stories are examples. For decisions about exchange or taxes, talk to an accountant.

## 4. Names and content

The Travessia name, brand and copy were created for this project. The integrations mentioned are fictional too.

## 5. Changes to these terms

These terms may change with the project. The version and date at the top show the latest.

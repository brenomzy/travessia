## 1. What data we collect

At a real company, this section would list every piece of data collected and where it comes from. At Travessia, the waitlist, newsletter and event forms take a name and email, show the success message and discard what was sent.

## 2. What we use it for

Nothing beyond demonstrating the form flow. No email is sent from this site.

## 3. Who we share it with

Nobody. The site is hosted by Webflow, which logs technical access data such as IP addresses under its own terms.

## 4. How long we keep it

Zero days. Form data is never stored.

## 5. Your rights under the LGPD

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data at any time. As this site stores no data, the answer to any request will be that nothing is on record.

## 6. How to reach us

At a real company, the data protection officer's contact would be here. For this project, questions go to whoever maintains the demo site.

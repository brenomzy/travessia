## Nobody gets the best day right

The exchange rate moves every day, and predicting it is a job for people who do it for a living. When you're paid from abroad, the goal is different: don't convert everything at the worst moment.

## Three simple rules

1. Convert what you need for the month as soon as the money lands.
2. Leave the rest in your dollar balance and set a rate alert.
3. When the alert goes off, convert part of it, not all of it.

That way you pay your bills without depending on the day's rate, and still catch the highs when they come.

## The minimum your client's finance team needs

An international invoice has no official format, but your client's finance team will always look for the same things: who's billing, who's paying, what was done, how much and by when.

1. Your legal name and company number (CNPJ), if you have one.
2. Your client's company name and address.
3. A short description of the work and the period.
4. The amount, the currency and the due date.

## What usually delays payment

Brazilian bank details in the body of the invoice. Your client won't know what to do with a Brazilian branch and account number. A payment link with card or local transfer solves it.

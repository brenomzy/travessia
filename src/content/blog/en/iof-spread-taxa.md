## The three costs

When money from abroad turns into reais, three things can shrink it: the fee of whoever converts it, the spread (the gap between the commercial rate and the one you get) and, in some cases, IOF, a Brazilian tax on financial transactions.

## An example with US$ 2,000

| Cost | Bank | Travessia |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Receiving fee | US$ 45.00 | US$ 0.00 |
| Spread on the rate | 4% | 0% |
| Conversion fee | – | 0.6% |
| Arrives in reais | R$ 10,228.56 | R$ 10,834.60 |

Example values, at a commercial rate of R$ 5.45.

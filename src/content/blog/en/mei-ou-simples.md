## MEI is simple, up to the limit

An MEI pays a fixed amount every month and has almost no paperwork. The catch is the yearly revenue limit: with clients paying in dollars, you reach it fast.

## Simples costs more, and allows more

Under Simples Nacional, the tax is a percentage of revenue that depends on your activity and the year's total. In return, the limit is much higher and you can have partners and employees.

## How to decide

Add up what you received over the last twelve months. If you're already past 80% of the MEI limit, it's time to talk to your accountant about switching.

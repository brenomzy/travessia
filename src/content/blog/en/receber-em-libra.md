## What changed

From today, your account has a pound balance next to dollars and euros. Clients in the UK pay by local transfer, with a British sort code and account number.

## How to use it

When you create an invoice, pick pounds as the currency. The payment link shows your client the right details, and the money lands in your GBP balance.

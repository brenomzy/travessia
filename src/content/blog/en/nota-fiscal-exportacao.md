## What it is

The export nota fiscal records a service provided to someone abroad. It's issued on your city hall's portal like any service invoice, with the foreign client as the buyer.

## The fields people get wrong

1. The service code, which has to match your activity.
2. The client's country and full address.
3. The value in reais, converted on the right date.

## What to keep

Keep the invoice, the payment receipt and the tax invoice's XML file. That's what your accountant will ask for at the end of the month.

# Illustration and 3D test (playbook step 10, 2026-11-24)

One test of each option on the same three jobs: an app empty state, a three-step "how it works", and a 4:5 launch post. Board: Paper page "Brand · Illustration and 3D" → "1 · Three options, three jobs".

- **3D** (`3d/`): six renders from Magnific, Nano Banana 2.1 at 1k, 300 credits. Two takes each of a floating invoice sheet (1a, 1b), a phone with a blank igarapé screen (2a, 2b), and an invoice folded into a paper boat crossing from dark water into clear green-teal (3a, 3b).
- **Flat illustration**: drawn as SVG directly on the Paper board in palette colours (invoice card, route arc, river segments, phone, check, clock). No files here.
- **Neither**: built on the board from what the brand already has: a Hugeicons-style icon, product UI with ledger numbers on the paintings, and the clear-water painting for the post.

Prompts (each ended with "no logos, no money, no people, no readable text"):

- Invoice: a clean 3D render of a single paper invoice sheet floating at a slight angle, one corner curled, on deep blue-black (#0F1C24), faint grey lines for text, a round green-teal check stamp (#00C99B), soft overcast studio light.
- Phone: a modern smartphone at a three-quarter angle, its screen plain glowing green-teal (#00C99B), matte dark body, on deep blue-black, lots of empty space on the left.
- Paper boat: a white paper invoice folded into a small paper boat on calm water seen from slightly above, deep blue-black on the left turning clear green-teal on the right, the boat crossing the line between them, a quiet dark top third.

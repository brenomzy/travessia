How the reference brands build effects

  • Stripe: the wave is a live WebGL canvas behind the hero, kept away from every control.
  • Mercury: a pre-rendered video that pushes from a landscape into the product, plus monochrome renders with film grain.
  • Ramp: rendered stills and video, with the yellow used as light behind product art.
  • Common thread: one effect per page, behind the product, never under body text.

What would ship

The site already has a WebGL2 seam engine (src/lib/generators/seam) with flow, drift, breathe and shimmer, a PNG still for reduced motion and no-JS, and one canvas per page. A chosen effect here becomes a second recipe in that engine, not a new library.

Rules any pick has to keep

  • Two waters: a dark side and a light side, or a dark field and a light river.
  • Clean, clear water: no murky browns.
  • Still frame under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Calm speed: one full change takes tens of seconds, not seconds.
